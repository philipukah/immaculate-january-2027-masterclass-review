Why this masterclass exists

The UK process is easier to navigate when you understand the moving parts.

Most applicants first see a course advert, a success story or a low tuition figure. Then they start looking for somebody to “process” everything.

The real work comes later: course direction, academic fit, documents, deposits, finances, CAS requirements and visa-stage preparation. Those decisions are personal to your profile.

This session gives you the foundation to ask better questions and see why an individual assessment comes before serious work starts.
